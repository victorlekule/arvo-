04 The Arvo Signature

What makes an ARVO piece,
an ARVO piece.

A signature is not a logo. It is a standard — the set of decisions that stay constant no matter what we make. Five principles define every piece that carries our name.

01

Textile · The Material

Chosen by hand, before anything else is designed.

Every ARVO piece begins with the textile. We work with kitenge, batiki, kanga and a small library of natural fibres — seagrass, sisal, water hyacinth and raffia — selected for hand-feel, colour depth and how they age with use. Nothing is ordered from a catalogue. Everything is chosen in person, in the studio, by the people who will make it.

  • Kitenge
  • Batiki
  • Kanga
  • Seagrass
  • Sisal
  • Water Hyacinth
  • Raffia

Craft · The Hand

Made by named artisans, not anonymous factories.

Our pieces are woven, dyed, cut and finished by hand in Tanzania. We work with weavers, tailors and beadworkers whose techniques have been passed down for generations — and we credit them by name. No piece leaves the studio without a maker's mark and a final inspection.

  • Handwoven
  • Hand-dyed
  • Hand-finished
  • Named Artisans
  • Small Batch
02
03

Form · The Shape

Heritage soul. Contemporary cut.

We do not reproduce traditional forms — we reinterpret them. Every silhouette is developed for how people actually dress and live today: structured blazers, relaxed co-ords, clean-lined baskets, functional bags. The heritage lives in the material and the making. The form is entirely of the present.

  • Modern Tailoring
  • Functional Shapes
  • Structured Cuts
  • Contemporary Lines

Detail · The Decision

The difference lives in the smallest choices.

A contrasting Ankara lining inside a seagrass basket. A reinforced strap where the bag carries weight. A hand-rolled edge on a cushion. These are the decisions that separate a considered piece from a manufactured one — and they are the ones you only notice when you live with the piece.

  • Contrast Lining
  • Hand-rolled Edges
  • Reinforced Stitching
  • Beadwork
  • Finished Seams
04
05

Purpose · The Life

Designed to be used, worn, and lived with.

An ARVO piece is not an ornament. It is made to be carried every day, worn through seasons, filled and emptied, gifted and re-gifted. We design for longevity — in construction, in materials and in style — so that what you buy today is still what you reach for in ten years.

  • Built for Daily Use
  • Made to Last
  • Timeless by Design
  • Repairable
05 Materials & Textiles

The materials
behind every piece.

What a piece is made from matters more than what it looks like. Here is the honest reference — where each material comes from, how we use it, and how to care for it so it lasts.

Printed Cotton

Kitenge

A vibrant East African wax-print cotton, worn for celebration and daily life alike. Bold pattern, saturated colour, and a hand-feel that softens with every wear.

Origin East & Central Africa

Best for Apparel, linings, cushions, gift items

Care Hand wash cold · dry in shade

Hand-Dyed Textile

Batiki

Hand-dyed viscose-cotton with soft, contemporary patterns. Each length is unique — the dye never lands exactly the same way twice, which is what makes it unmistakable.

Origin Tanzania & East Africa

Best for Dresses, shirts, co-ords, blazers

Care Hand wash cold · iron inside out

Wax Print Cotton

Ankara

A bold West African wax print, known for graphic patterns and strong colour. We use Ankara most often as a lining or accent — the surprise detail inside a piece.

Origin West Africa

Best for Lining, trim, statement details

Care Hand wash cold · colour-safe detergent

Printed Wrap Cloth

Kanga

A lightweight printed cloth woven with a Swahili proverb along its border. Traditionally worn and gifted across East Africa — every Kanga carries a message.

Origin Tanzania & Kenya

Best for Pillowcases, wraps, gift pieces

Care Hand wash cold · dry in shade

Soft Blend Fabric

Viscose Cotton

A soft, breathable blend of viscose and cotton. It drapes beautifully, holds colour deeply, and stays comfortable in warm climates — the base fabric for most of our bespoke wear.

Origin Blended · dyed in East Africa

Best for Dresses, blouses, co-ords, kimonos

Care Hand wash cold · hang dry

Natural Fibre

Cotton

The foundation fibre of most of our fabric. Breathable, durable, and forgiving — cotton takes dye beautifully and softens with age, which is why we trust it for everyday pieces.

Origin Regionally sourced

Best for Everyday wear, linings, all fabric blends

Care Machine wash cool · iron warm

Woven Fibre

Seagrass

A natural, renewable fibre harvested and dried by hand, then woven into baskets and rugs. Seagrass is strong, subtly fragrant when new, and ages into a warm, lived-in tone.

Origin Coastal Tanzania

Best for Baskets, hampers, rugs, storage

Care Dust dry · keep away from damp

Agave Fibre

Sisal

A strong, durable fibre extracted from the agave plant. Sisal is coarse, hard-wearing and naturally resistant — the fibre we choose when a piece needs to hold its shape for years.

Origin East Africa

Best for Rugs, mats, sturdy baskets

Care Vacuum gently · spot clean

Aquatic Fibre

Water Hyacinth

A fast-growing aquatic plant, harvested and dried into a soft, pliable fibre. Working with it keeps waterways clear — a material that is both beautiful and restorative to its source.

Origin Lake regions of East Africa

Best for Storage boxes, organisers, decor

Care Dust dry · keep away from moisture

Palm Fibre

Raffia

A flexible, lightweight fibre stripped from the raffia palm. Soft to the touch, strong under tension, and naturally pale — the fibre behind our Kapu baskets and hand fans.

Origin Madagascar & East Africa

Best for Baskets, fans, decorative accents

Care Dust dry · reshape by hand

Embellishment

Beads

Glass and Maasai beadwork, threaded by hand one bead at a time. Beads add colour, texture and meaning — often carrying symbolic patterns specific to the artisan who made them.

Origin Tanzania & Kenya

Best for Accents, organisers, keepsakes

Care Dust with soft cloth · avoid impact

A note on materials

Every material we use is chosen for how it wears, not just how it looks — and every piece comes with a care note.
