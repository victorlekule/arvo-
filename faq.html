Orders & Payments

How do I place an order?

Simply browse our shop, add items to your cart, and proceed to checkout. You'll receive an instant confirmation email with your order number. We accept Visa, Mastercard, PayPal and M-Pesa.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay, and M-Pesa. All transactions are SSL-encrypted and processed through PCI-compliant providers.

Can I cancel or change my order?

Yes — if your order hasn't been shipped yet. Contact us within 12 hours of placing your order and we'll happily adjust or cancel it. Once dispatched, standard returns apply.

Shipping & Delivery

Do you ship worldwide?

Yes — we ship to 24 countries. Tracked delivery is available at checkout. Shipping is free on orders over $150. Duties and taxes are calculated at checkout for most destinations.

How long does delivery take?

Domestic (Tanzania): 2–4 business days. Regional (East Africa): 5–8 days. International: 7–14 days for stock items. Bespoke pieces add 10–14 days for production.

Can I track my order?

Yes — as soon as your order ships you'll receive a tracking link by email. You can also log into your Arvo account anytime to see live updates.

Returns & Exchanges

What is your return policy?

Stock items can be returned within 30 days in original, unused condition for a full refund. Bespoke and custom-made pieces are final sale. See our full Refund Policy for complete details.

How do I start a return?

Email support@arvostyles.com with your order number. We'll send a prepaid return label and step-by-step instructions within 24 hours.

Bespoke & Custom Orders

How does the bespoke process work?

Every bespoke piece begins with a virtual or in-person fitting. We discuss fabric, fit, and details, then send you a quote and timeline. 50% deposit confirms your order; balance due at dispatch.

How long does a bespoke order take?

Most bespoke pieces are delivered within 14–21 days. Rush orders can sometimes be arranged — just tell us your deadline when you book.

Do you offer corporate and bulk orders?

Absolutely. We offer corporate gifting and wholesale pricing from 10 pieces upward — including custom branding, packaging and delivery logistics. Contact us for a quote.

Products & Care

How do I care for handcrafted leather?

Keep leather away from prolonged moisture and direct heat. Condition every 3–4 months with a natural leather balm. Store in the dust bag we provide when not in use.

How do I clean woven textiles?

Sisal and seagrass rugs: vacuum weekly, rotate every 3 months, spot-clean with a damp cloth. Kitenge and batiki garments: cool hand-wash, line-dry in shade.

Are your materials ethically sourced?

Yes. Every material we use is either certified fair-trade or sourced directly from small artisan communities we've personally visited. We publish our full materials list on request.
