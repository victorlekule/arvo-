About Arvo

Where heritage meets modern living.

Arvo Styles began with a simple belief: that the objects we live with should mean something. What started as a small collection of hand-stitched bags from a workshop in Dar es Salaam has grown into a house of curated craft — connecting over 150 artisans across Tanzania, Kenya and Rwanda with people who value authenticity.

Every piece we carry is designed in-house and made by hand. From full-grain leather bags to kitenge blazers, sisal rugs to hand-carved wall art — each object is a bridge between the hands that made it and the life it will live in.

We don't chase trends. We build heirlooms.

Amani N.

Our Mission

To celebrate African craftsmanship by bringing handcrafted, ethically made pieces to modern homes around the world — while ensuring the artisans behind every stitch earn a fair, dignified living from their work.

To become Africa's most trusted house of heritage-inspired living — where the word "handmade" is never a compromise, and where modern luxury and traditional craft are one and the same thing.
