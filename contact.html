Frequently Asked

Questions before you write to us?

You might find the answer here — the fastest way to solve most things.

How long does a bespoke order take?

Most bespoke pieces are delivered within 14–21 days from the moment we receive your measurements and full payment. Rush orders can sometimes be arranged — just ask.

Do you ship worldwide?

Yes — we ship to 24 countries with tracked delivery. Shipping is free on orders over $150. Duties and taxes are calculated at checkout for most destinations.

Can I order in bulk for my company?

Absolutely. We offer corporate gifting and wholesale pricing from 10 pieces upward. Select "Wholesale / Corporate" in the contact form and tell us more about your order.

What is your returns policy?

Stock items can be returned within 30 days in original condition for a full refund. Bespoke and custom-made pieces are final sale — but we'll always work with you on adjustments.

How do I care for my handcrafted piece?

Every piece arrives with a care card. In short: leather loves leather balm, natural fibres like a gentle vacuum, and kitenge likes a cool wash. We're happy to advise specific pieces.
