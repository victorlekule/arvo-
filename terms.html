Refund & Returns Policy

We want you to love what you buy from Arvo Styles. If something isn't right, we'll do our best to make it right. This policy explains how refunds, returns, and exchanges work.

1. Return Window

You may return most stock items within 30 days of receiving your order, provided they are:

  • Unused and in the same condition you received them
  • In their original packaging with tags attached
  • Accompanied by proof of purchase

2. Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned or refunded:

  • Bespoke and custom-made pieces
  • Personalised or monogrammed items
  • Intimate apparel and used personal items
  • Items marked "Final Sale" at checkout

3. How to Start a Return

Email support@arvostyles.com with:

  • Your order number
  • The item(s) you wish to return
  • The reason for return

We'll respond within 24 hours with a prepaid return label and clear instructions.

4. Refund Timeline

Once we receive and inspect your return, we'll process the refund within 5–7 business days. Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Depending on your bank, it may take an additional 3–5 days to appear.

5. Damaged or Incorrect Items

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, contact us within 48 hours of delivery. We'll arrange a replacement or full refund at no cost to you — including return shipping.

6. Exchanges

We're happy to exchange items for a different size or colour, subject to availability. Exchanges follow the same 30-day window and process as returns.

7. Bespoke Orders

Because bespoke pieces are made specifically for you, they're non-refundable once production has started. However, we'll always work with you on fit adjustments after delivery within reasonable means.

8. Questions?

Not sure if your item qualifies? Just ask. Email support@arvostyles.com and we'll help you figure it out.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your orders

We use cookies to remember your cart, keep you logged in, and understand how people navigate the site. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work as intended.

10. Changes to This Policy
